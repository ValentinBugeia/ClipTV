Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Summary

ClipTV runs on your own computer. It has no server and no user accounts. Your data stays on your computer and is never sold or shared, except with the services you connect (TikTok, Twitch and, if you choose, Anthropic for writing captions), only to do what you ask.

Data ClipTV uses

From TikTok, after you connect your account and only with the permissions you grant:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, shown so you know which account you are publishing to;
  • video.upload and video.publish: to send the videos you approved to your account;
  • user.info.stats and video.list: your follower and like counts and the list of your videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, shown on ClipTV's Statistics page.

ClipTV also uses the creator information returned by TikTok (available privacy options, interaction settings, maximum video length) to show the right choices before you publish.

From Twitch: public clips and stream information (titles, view counts, channel names) used to find and credit highlights.

Created by ClipTV: the edited videos, subtitles, captions and publishing schedule.

Where data is stored

Everything, including the TikTok access and refresh tokens, is stored in ClipTV's data folder on your computer. ClipTV's developers do not receive or have access to it.

Sharing

  • TikTok receives the videos and captions you publish, and the requests needed to read your statistics.
  • Twitch receives the requests needed to list and download public clips.
  • Anthropic (optional, only if you add your own Claude API key) receives the clip title and transcript to suggest a caption.

No data is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove everything: delete ClipTV's data folder, and revoke ClipTV's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable immediately.

Children

ClipTV is not intended for people under 18.
